# NESBA company brief (shared by every department skill)

**What NESBA is:** National Entrepreneurship & Small Business Academy. A learning platform and community for entrepreneurs: practical business courses, live classes and workshops in a 3D lecture hall, 1-to-1 mentoring, project groups, certificates, and verified organization pages. Website and app (Expo, iOS, Android, web) at https://nesba.app.

**Mission:** Give every entrepreneur, wherever they start, practical skills, real mentors and a community to build with.

**Where:** Operates from the Netherlands (EU VAT, iDEAL, GDPR/AVG, KVK). Later (2030–2031) English-speaking creators and US small-business audiences.

**Who it is for:**
- Starting founders: self-employed or about to start, for example a Dutch zzp'er.
- Creators: teachers, mentors, accountants, marketers who want an audience, fair earnings and one tool for courses, live classes and bookings.
- Organizations that support entrepreneurs: startup programs, universities, business associations (including Arabic-, Turkish- and Moroccan-Dutch associations), municipalities.

**Prices (before VAT):** Course about €49. 1-to-1 session (60 min) about €60. NESBA Pro membership €9.99/month. Many starter courses, open live classes, project groups and certificates are free.

**Creator deal:** creators keep 80% of every sale; NESBA keeps 20%. Payments through Stripe Connect, on the web only (the app unlocks what was bought; no Apple/Google in-app purchases). Launch offer: free setup support, a free launch live event, featured placement for the first 50 creators. Zzp'ers can run one paid masterclass without building a course.

**Growth so far:** marketing budget €0. Free channels: KVK and Ondernemersplein events, startup ecosystems (Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Utrecht), university entrepreneurship centers, community associations, the founder's LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok. Built in: invite-a-friend links with badges, shareable certificates (LinkedIn), the launch waitlist at https://nesba.app/waitlist (beehiiv newsletter; add `?ref=<channel>` to track where sign-ups come from).

**Brand:** logo "NESBA USA" (keep it as it is). Main colour purple #6C5CE7 (dark mode) / #5F4FDC (light). Tone: practical, warm, direct, no hype; "people who have built a business teach you". Trust: privacy by design, fair prices, verified teachers and organizations, no ads, no selling data.

**Ground rules for every department:**
- Never invent numbers (users, revenue, testimonials, partners). Mark assumptions as assumptions.
- Never paste API keys or passwords in chat; secrets go in the Supabase dashboard.
- Anything legal, tax or financial that will be relied on must be checked by a qualified Dutch professional (accountant, lawyer) before use.
- Business plan, roadmap and app-tour videos live in `docs/` and are deliberately not committed to git.
- Write in plain language. The founder is not technical.
